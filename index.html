    Install Ream

    Add it to your Home Screen to open it like a normal app — it then works fully offline.

    1. Tap the Share button in the browser toolbar.
    2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add, then launch it from your Home Screen.
    1. Chrome or Edge: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose Install Ream.
    2. Safari on a Mac: choose File → Add to Dock.
    3. Open Ream from your apps — it runs offline from then on.
